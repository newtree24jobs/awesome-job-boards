# Remote job search checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate remote technology vacancies before applying.

1. Confirm the employer and requisition on its official careers website.
2. Check eligible countries, time zones and work authorization; remote does not necessarily mean worldwide.
3. Record the role, employer, application URL, deadline and status in a spreadsheet.
4. Tailor your resume to the actual requirements and include measurable accomplishments.
5. Never pay an application fee to an unverified recruiter.
